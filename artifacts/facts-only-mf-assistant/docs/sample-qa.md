# Sample questions and sourced answers

The factual examples below were checked through the **live Groq/Chroma answer path** using the HDFC source snapshot dated **27 September 2026**. Refusal examples were checked before any model call. Wording can vary between runs. Do not treat time-varying figures as current.

1. **What is the lock-in period of HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?**  
   The lock-in period is **3 years**. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund — Direct Plan](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct)

2. **What is the minimum SIP for HDFC Small Cap Fund?**  
   **₹100**. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC Small Cap Fund — Direct Plan](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund/direct)

3. **Which asset classes does HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund invest in?**  
   It invests in a mix of **equity and debt** instruments; the allocation is determined by the fund manager according to prevailing market and economic conditions. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund — Direct Plan](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund/direct)

4. **What benchmark does HDFC Flexi Cap Fund use?**  
   **NIFTY 500 Total Returns Index**. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC Flexi Cap Fund — Direct Plan](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct)

5. **Where can I get an account statement?**  
   Open HDFC Mutual Fund's official **Download Statement** page. An account statement is available after investing and the service says there is no charge. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC Mutual Fund — Download Account Statement](https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/consolidated-account-statement)

6. **How do I download a capital gains statement?**  
   The HDFC guide describes obtaining a report for a selected financial year from a registrar, a platform's tax/reports section, or the AMC website. It also describes the capital-gains format for a consolidated statement. Last updated from sources: 2026-09-27.  
   Source: [HDFC Mutual Fund — Capital Gain Statement guide](https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india)
